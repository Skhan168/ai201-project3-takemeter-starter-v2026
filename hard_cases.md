# Hard Cases

## 1. Personal experience vs. recommendation
Text: My Hero Academia taught me that hard work can lead to greatness.
Label: personal_experience
Reason: It describes what the writer personally learned, rather than advising someone else to watch it.

## 2. Title-only vs. recommendation
Text: Spy x Family
Label: title_only
Reason: The comment names an anime but gives no opinion, reason, or personal experience.

## 3. Reasoned recommendation
Text: Watch Frieren because its slow pacing and emotional story make the characters feel realistic.
Label: reasoned_recommendation
Reason: It recommends an anime and provides specific supporting reasons.
